Cabin Assignment is a room assignment tool that allows you to configure a group of people and a set of rooms / houses with bed counts and pull out sofas, etc. It's great for getting a quick sense of who is staying where and can be used for group Airbnb retreats. I built it because...

I'm getting married! My fianceé and I are reserving cabin lodges that can house multiple groups of guests, so lodging isn't in a typical hotel structure. Because of that, we needed to figure out which cabins had enough beds for certain groups of people.

I quickly drafted up Cabin Assignment that allows you to upload whoever is attending your event, add the rooms/houses that you have, and drag and drop between the two to organize who stays where.

Note: the data stays local to your browser, so if you upload a specific guest/lodging configuration on your laptop, you can't really share it with anyone else, unless you use the "Backup" feature in the toolbar.
